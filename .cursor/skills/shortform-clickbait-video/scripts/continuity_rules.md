# Continuity directive block + negative library (append to EVERY shot prompt)

These encode the source brief's HARD CONSTRAINTS as reusable prompt directives. Append the block
below to each shot's compiled prompt, then add the shot's own **Continuity Notes** cell and the
explicit **screen-direction / eyeline** for that beat.

## Shared continuity block (paste into every shot)
> Continuity: keep the SAME location, environment, architecture, terrain, spatial layout, weather,
> time of day, props and background as the neighbouring shots — only camera framing and character
> action change. Keep character appearance, wardrobe, mood and lighting identical to the supplied
> references across all shots; no drift. Maintain the established 180-degree line and screen
> direction; do not flip gaze, head orientation or body facing unless the sheet specifies it.
> Continue the current action and (for spoken lines) the current sentence seamlessly from the
> previous shot — preserve body momentum, pose, hand positions, expression, timing and lip movement;
> do not restart, repeat or skip. Follow the storyboard exactly: only the specified shot, camera
> move, action and emotional beat — invent no new events, characters, props or angles. Cinematic
> composition, depth and lighting with punchy short-form hook energy. Output 1:1, 720x720.

## Per-shot screen-direction / eyeline (fill from the beat; example bible for a two-hander)
- Dominant character on screen-right, higher; looks DOWN toward screen-left.
- Lower/kneeling character on screen-left; looks UP toward screen-right. (Makes 17↔18 a matched reverse.)
- Fire / key light spills from screen-right; keep it consistent shot to shot.
- One hero prop (e.g. the sealed Black Book) — identical design every shot; never opened.
- Crowd/extras in a blurred semicircle behind on screen-right.

## Lip-sync vs V.O.
- Spoken line (e.g. `MAX: ...`): instruct accurate natural mouth movement to that exact line in its
  language; keep head movement minimal so the visemes read.
- Voice-over (`(V.O.)`): mouth stays closed except natural breathing.

## Baked negative library (append to every shot)
`no subtitles, no captions, no watermarks, no logos, no on-screen text, no duplicated cast likenesses,
no extreme eyeball close-up, no disembodied or glowing eye, no glowing orb, no floating lights,
no opening the sealed book, no gore, no extra limbs, no identity drift`

effortlessly, flawlessly, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, next-level, rich tapestry, kaleidoscope of,
